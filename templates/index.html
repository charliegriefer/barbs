Your New Best Friend Might Be Waiting Right Here.

Big dogs, little dogs. Puppies, seniors, and everything in between. With dogs available for adoption at both our Rocky Point, Mexico rescue and our adoption center in Peoria, Arizona, there are so many incredible dogs waiting to meet their people.
Don't let location stop you from falling in love! Click on a dog's photo to see where they are currently located. If your perfect match is in Rocky Point, we can often help arrange transportation to our Peoria location.
Every dog at Barb's Dog Rescue has a story. Some were surrendered by families who could no longer care for them. Many were rescued from life on the streets. Others came to us sick, injured, neglected, or abused. Their stories may be different, but today they all want the same thing: a family to call their own.
Our Rocky Point rescue is just about an hour south of the Lukeville, Arizona border and an easy drive from the Phoenix area. Adopting a Mexi-dog and bringing them home to the U.S. is easier than you might think—we'll help provide what you need for the journey home.
Prefer to adopt closer to home? Come meet our dogs at 8028 W. Thunderbird Rd., Peoria, AZ.
